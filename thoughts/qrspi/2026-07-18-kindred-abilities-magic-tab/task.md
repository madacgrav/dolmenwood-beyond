# Task

Elf/Grimalkin innate glamours should be randomly assigned during character creation (currently they can only be added manually after the fact, never rolled at creation). Additionally, other kindred special abilities — Woodgrue Mad Revelry, Grimalkin Shape-Shifting, Mossling Knacks — are not surfaced on the character sheet's Magic tab and should be. Open design question: whether the "Magic" tab should be renamed to something more inclusive of these non-spell abilities.
